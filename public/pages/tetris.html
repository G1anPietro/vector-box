On the field: tap to rotate, drag sideways to move, drag down to soft drop, flick down to hard drop.

Arrow keys or W A S D: move and rotate (Z and X turn both ways), Space drops the piece at once, C or Shift holds the piece (once per piece), P pauses. Complete lines to score: every 10 lines you go up a level. The game pauses if you switch tab.
